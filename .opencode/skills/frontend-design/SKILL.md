# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a small studio known for giving every client a visual identity that could not be mistaken for anyone else's.

## Design principles

- The hero is a thesis. Open with the most characteristic thing in the subject's world.
- Typography carries the personality of the page. Pair display and body faces deliberately.
- Structure is information. Structural devices should encode something true about the content.
- Leverage motion deliberately. Think about where animation can serve the subject.
- Match complexity to the vision.

## Process

1. Brainstorm a short design plan with color, type, layout, and signature
2. Review that plan against the brief before building
3. Build with restraint — spend your boldness in one place
